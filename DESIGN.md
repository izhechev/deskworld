# DeskWorld design

## The city board

The first screen is the playable neighbourhood. The 3D model receives most of the width, with a shelf of building pieces beside it. On a phone, the board comes first and the shelf follows directly below. Keep free play available as soon as the page loads.

## Colour and type

Use warm paper for the work surface, blue-black for the header and labels, and petrol blue for the storm panel. Yellow marks the selected tool and the challenge action. Match piece colours to the city model where possible. Use Barlow Condensed for headings and counters, with system text for controls.

## Interaction

Keep the selected piece visible and give buttons a short press response. The map, keyboard shortcuts and paper demo share the same building tools. Use the canvas to show changes to the city and reserve interface motion for feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
