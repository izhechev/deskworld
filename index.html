How to play

Build a neighbourhood and watch twelve neighbours walk from studios to homes.

  1. Pick a piece. Click a tile to place it, or use the keyboard map.
  2. Connect homes to studios. People walk only along roads and cannot cut through other buildings.
  3. Bring some paper. Coral makes a home, green makes a park, and blue blocks a road.

For paper tracking, point your camera straight down at a plain square board; align it with the preview square and keep the camera still.

A small simulation, inspired by tangible city research; it is not an urban planning forecast.

Beat the storm

Start with a neighbourhood split by three roadblocks on row 4.

Replace a roadblock with a road to reconnect the northern homes, then get all twelve neighbours home before the rain.

Your sandbox city is kept for your return; homes and studios stay fixed during the challenge.
